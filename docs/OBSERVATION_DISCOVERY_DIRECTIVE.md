# Observation Discovery Directive

**Effective:** 2026-07-29  
**Complements:** Alpha Discovery Directive, LEDA roadmap, Standing Research Director  
**Status:** Permanent research philosophy (not a trading rulebook)

## Shift

Do **not** optimise for:

- more indicators  
- more signal transforms of the same series  
- more long/flat rules on exhausted public levels  

Optimise for:

> **Discovering new measurable observations about markets** that reduce uncertainty under frozen scientific standards.

## Core questions (always on)

1. **What observable property of the market are we currently blind to?**  
2. **What information could reduce uncertainty rather than predict direction?**  
3. **What measurable phenomenon might explain why previous hypotheses failed?**  
4. **Which observations are independent from everything already tested?**  

## Preference order

| Prefer | Avoid |
|--------|--------|
| Genuinely new measurable property | Another SMA / mom / z-score of close |
| Observations that change the information set | Complexity that re-packages the same set |
| Characterization before trade mapping | Jumping to entry/exit expressions |
| Independence / ablation tests | Multi-parameter signal grids |
| Free reconstructible series first | Paid vendors without EIR |

## LEDA fit

Observations feed **evidence channels** (scores with PIT, skill claims).  
They do **not** automatically become trades. Portfolio/execution stay thin until confirmatory earns more.

## Success

Uncertainty reduction about *what the market is doing* — including high-quality nulls that retire a proposed observation family.

## Interrupt criteria (autonomous work)

Stop and surface to human when:

- a **genuinely new evidence family** is identified and locked for programme design  
- major architecture revision is scientifically justified  
- external dependency (API key, paid data, infra) blocks progress  
- a candidate survives full confirmatory pathway  

Otherwise continue autonomously under frozen gates.
