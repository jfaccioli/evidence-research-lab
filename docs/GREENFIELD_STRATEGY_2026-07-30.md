# Greenfield Strategy — Chief Scientist Decision Document

**Date:** 2026-07-30  
**Role:** Chief Scientist / Research Director  
**Mode:** Implementation **suspended** — decision document only  
**Horizon:** 5 years  
**Constitution (binding):** `docs/AQRO_CONSTITUTION.md` — **permanent principles; provisional hypotheses**  
**Objective (fixed):**

> Maximise the probability of responsibly managing real capital autonomously over a five-year horizon while maintaining scientific integrity.

**Method:** Start from scratch with Phase 1 knowledge. Do not defend prior roadmaps. Rank **revisable** capital-path hypotheses by EV. Opportunity cost of months and dollars is real.

**Human update:** AQRO identity **accepted**. Market-structure as permanent organisational spine **rejected**. Market-structure is **H1 (leading scientific hypothesis)** only — evidence can demote it.

## 1. Executive decision (single recommendation, provisional spine)

### We will not continue as a “BTC daily long/flat signal factory.”

That path has been **empirically expensive and low-yield**. Phase 1 is strong evidence that free public daily directional prediction under frozen gates is a **poor mountain face** for capital-ready autonomy.

### Recommended *provisional* capital-path hypothesis (H1 — not doctrine)

**Current leading hypothesis:** a risk-budgeted crypto **market-structure** sleeve class (funding, basis, related structure) has **higher expected value** for the permanent objective than continuing free directional discovery — **until evidence says otherwise**.

**Organisation form (permanent):** Autonomous Quantitative Research Organisation (**AQRO**) = evidence/rejection engine first; capital products only when sleeves survive gates.

| Layer | Commitment | Reversible? |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| **Identity AQRO** | Evidence engine + earned capital products | Human constitutional only |
| **H1 active test priority** | Market-structure / risk-premia **first in queue to test**, not forever spine | **Yes** — see constitution §5 |
| **Mandate for testing H1** | Requires human charter if vehicle ≠ current long/flat policy | Human |
| **BTC role** | Primary liquidity/risk asset initially | Yes |
| **Multi-sleeve (H4)** | Preference **after** ≥1 validated sleeve | Yes |
| **Validation** | Frozen scientific gates + capital ladder | Gates: scientific flaw only |
| **Dual-track OS** | Parallel product track | Yes if dilutes science |
| **Hard stop** | No sleeve clears confirmatory + 90d shadow within 18 months of *authorised* capital-path charter → freeze capital track | Yes |

**Why H1 ranks first *today* (not forever):**  
Literature and Phase 1 posteriors place more mass on structural premia than on free daily direction. That is a **Bayesian ranking**, not architecture.

**Why this is not doctrine:**  
If H1 fails its pre-registered tests under elevated costs, AQRO **must re-rank** (e.g. toward H3 options structure, revised directional, or capital-track freeze + OS). No design may embed “MS forever.”

## 2. What Phase 1 actually taught us (used as priors, not loyalty)

| Learning | Prior update |
|----------|----------------|
| Free OHLCV technicals mass-null under gates | **Very low** P(standalone daily technical α) |
| Free structural long/flat (ETF flow, stablecoin Δlog, miner revenue) null or fragile | **Low** P(easy free long/flat structural α) |
| Gates correctly kill sparse high-Sharpe teases | Validation methodology for **false edge rejection** is an asset |
| Lab/platform maturity high; alpha evidence low | Stack value ≠ edge value |
| Process risk: low-EV programmes burn option value | Throughput is not EV |
| Literature: carry / limits-to-arb / options hedging are real *phenomena* | Higher prior on **market-structure books** than on daily direction |

**Conclusion for greenfield design:** Do not spend the next five years re-proving that free daily direction is hard. Spend them building a **capital system on higher-prior edge classes**, or honestly stop capital ambitions.

## 3. Assumptions challenged (again)

### 3.1 BTC-only as proving ground

| Challenge | Decision |
|-----------|----------|
| BTC is the most efficient crypto; edges thinner; sample long | BTC remains **primary** for risk and liquidity |
| ETH / majors may diversify capacity and mechanisms | **Second sleeve asset class only after** one BTC-centric sleeve survives — not a way to launder failed BTC ideas |
| Non-crypto equities/FX | Valid for **OS product generalisation**, not year-1 capital book unless human redefines business |

**Ruling:** BTC-first, not BTC-forever-only. Proving ground = **crypto liquid complex**, starting BTC.

### 3.2 Long/flat as permanent trading mandate

| Challenge | Decision |
|-----------|----------|
| Long/flat forbids the natural expression of funding, basis, and many inventory edges | Keeping it permanent **lowers 5-year P(objective)** |
| No-leverage was good risk policy for personal capital | Keep **ruin constraints**; do **not** keep “long/flat only” as identity of science |
| Jurisdiction / product risk for leveraged crypto | Any derivatives vehicle requires **explicit risk envelope, venue list, loss caps**, not casual perps gambling |

**Ruling:** Long/flat is a **retired default research envelope**, not the end-state capital mandate. New capital mandate = **risk-budgeted market-structure book** (may include delta-neutral and limited short inventory *as research-approved sleeves*).

### 3.3 Standalone strategy vs portfolio / ensemble

| Challenge | Decision |
|-----------|----------|
| One rule rarely funds autonomy; institutions run **books** | **Multi-sleeve** with a risk allocator is the correct end architecture |
| Ensemble of weak edges can still fail multiplicity | Each sleeve must pass **family gates**; allocator is not a place to hide overfitting |

**Ruling:** Standalone discovery is a **legacy Phase-1 habit**. Target architecture is **few validated sleeves + risk allocation**.

### 3.4 Is directional prediction the correct objective?

| Challenge | Decision |
|-----------|----------|
| Predicting E[r] daily is the hardest, most competed problem | Direction remains **one** possible sleeve class, not the objective |
| Capital cares about **compound growth under constraints** | Objective = **risk-adjusted capital process**, which may earn from premia, carry, mean-reversion of basis, gamma regimes — not only sign(BTC) |

**Ruling:** **No.** Directional prediction is demoted from *objective* to *optional sleeve type*.

### 3.5 Market structure, execution, allocation vs signal discovery

| Challenge | Decision |
|-----------|----------|
| Phase 1 over-weighted signal discovery | Correct diagnosis |
| Without edge, perfect execution is futile | Still need **some** edge class with prior > ~0 |
| Without risk/allocation/execution, edge dies in production | Co-equal layers |

**Ruling:** Priority order for the next 24 months:

1. **Mandate + risk system for a market-structure book**  
2. **First sleeve science** on highest-prior free/cheap data (funding/basis/premium we largely have)  
3. **Execution + shadow + ops** for that sleeve  
4. **Paid structural data** (options OI/gamma) only if earned by (2) or clearly higher EV than next free sleeve  
5. Directional long/flat free discovery — **deprioritised**

### 3.6 Evolve into broader autonomous quant research organisation?

| Challenge | Decision |
|-----------|----------|
| “Trading bot project” identity encourages forced alpha | Yes — rebrand mission to **AQRO** |
| Broader org without capital focus becomes consulting theatre | Capital objective remains; OS product is dual-track, not a substitute for honesty about α |

**Ruling:** **Yes.** The organisation’s identity should be **autonomous quantitative research with capital products**, not “find a BTC SMA.”

## 6. The recommended 5-year programme (greenfield design)

*Not implementation. This is the strategy map.*

### Identity

**Autonomous Quantitative Research Organisation (AQRO)**  
Product 1: **Evidence engine** (always on)  
Product 2: **Risk-budgeted capital book** (earned, not assumed)

### Capital mandate (to replace long/flat-as-success)

> Maximise long-run capital subject to: max drawdown bound, max gross exposure, venue concentration limits, no unbounded leverage, full audit trail, human kill-switch, and **no sleeve without confirmatory-grade evidence under frozen gates**.

### Year 0–0.5 — Charter and first sleeve

1. Human adopts **capital mandate** + risk envelope (legal/venue check).  
2. Retire “long/flat free discovery” as primary KPI.  
3. Open **one** market-structure sleeve programme on **existing** data (funding/basis/premium class) with ≤4 explor / ≤1 conf.  
4. Parallel: OS documentation / reliability dual-track (no conf budget).  

**Go/no-go A:** confirmatory fail → either second free MS sleeve **or** paid options structural test **or** freeze capital path (human). Max two MS attempts before paid data.

### Year 0.5–1.5 — Shadow and micro-capital

5. Survivor → live-data shadow + ops readiness + cost audit.  
6. Human micro-capital open only after shadow.  
7. Kill-switch drills; minimal intervention = automated risk + alerting, not unattended hope.  

**Go/no-go B (18 months from charter):** no micro-capital-stable sleeve → **stop capital autonomy track**; OS-only.

### Year 1.5–3 — Second sleeve + allocation

8. Only if first sleeve stable: add second **orthogonal** sleeve (e.g. options structure **or** ETH MS check — not a technical clone).  
9. Introduce **explicit risk allocator** across sleeves (rules-based, pre-reg).  
10. Capacity and fee sensitivity continuous tests.  

### Year 3–5 — Minimal-intervention autonomy

11. Reduce human touchpoints to exception handling.  
12. Scale notionals only with capacity evidence.  
13. Optional generalise OS commercially; trading remains evidence-gated.  

### Explicit non-goals (5 years)

- Winning Kaggle-style backtests  
- NN/RL as the hypothesis  
- Daily technical clone grids  
- Unbounded multi-asset expansion  
- Claiming autonomy without shadow + micro-capital ladder
