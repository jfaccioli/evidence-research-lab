# Mechanism Discovery Directive

**Effective:** 2026-07-29  
**Primary mission mode** for the laboratory after LEDA scaffold maturity and property-characterization maturity.  
**Subordinates:** Market Properties Directive (properties are instruments of mechanisms, not the goal).

## Objective

Maximise the probability of discovering **genuinely new market mechanisms** —
economic or behavioural processes that generate observed market behaviour.

Do **not** optimise for:

- number of characterised properties  
- dataset acquisition for its own sake  
- architectural novelty  

## Workflow (mandatory order)

```text
Economic / behavioural mechanism
  → Observable market properties (minimum set)
  → Characterisation
  → Independence
  → Candidate evidence channel (optional)
  → LEDA (optional)
  → Forecast / risk claim
  → Decision (only under frozen gates)
```

**Never** begin with “what free dataset can we add?”  
**Always** begin with an **unanswered scientific question**.

## Questions that define mechanisms (examples)

- How is liquidity created, transferred and destroyed?  
- How does leverage build and unwind?  
- How do participants change behaviour under uncertainty?  
- How does information propagate across markets?  
- What processes cause **volatility clustering**?  
- What mechanisms explain **persistent market dislocations**?  

## For every proposed mechanism

1. State the **economic / behavioural hypothesis**.  
2. List the **minimum observables** needed to study it.  
3. Mark which already **exist** in the lab.  
4. Mark which are **missing**.  
5. Estimate **expected scientific value** *before* collecting new data.  
6. Prefer studies that use existing properties first.  

## Path to LEDA

Only after the mechanism is articulated, observables characterised, and a
**falsifiable claim** is pre-registered. Properties do not automatically become
channels. Composites only under synthesis rules (pre-reg target, simple rule,
family trial cost, ablation).

## Interrupt criteria

Interrupt the human only when:

- a **previously unmodelled mechanism** is identified as scientifically promising;  
- an **external resource** is required to investigate it;  
- a **full LEDA pathway** earns promotion under existing gates.  

## Success

Uncertainty reduction about *why* markets behave as they do — including high-quality
nulls that retire a mechanism operationalisation.
