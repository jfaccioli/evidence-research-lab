# Market Properties Directive

**Effective:** 2026-07-29  
**Primary mission now:** `docs/MECHANISM_DISCOVERY_DIRECTIVE.md`  
Properties are **instruments for studying mechanisms**, not an end in themselves.  
**Complements:** Alpha Discovery, LEDA, Standing Research Director  

## Objective

Support mechanism science with well-characterised, independent observables of market behaviour.

Not:

- indicator discovery  
- trading-signal search  
- parameter fishing on known series  

Yes:

- discovering and **characterising market properties**  
- understanding mechanisms, independence, and roles  
- reducing uncertainty about how the market works  

## Questions every new property must answer first

1. **What does this property actually measure?**  
2. **What economic or behavioural mechanism could explain it?**  
3. **How independent is it from properties already known?**  
4. **Does it primarily speak to direction, uncertainty, liquidity, participation, structure, or something else?**  
5. **Under which conditions does it become informative?**  
6. **Does it improve understanding even if it never produces a trading edge?**  

## Path to LEDA

```text
discover property → characterise → document → (optional) pre-reg evidence channel → frozen gates
```

**Never:** property → trade rule without characterization.

A high-quality null characterization is success (uncertainty reduced).

## Preference

| Prefer | Avoid |
|--------|--------|
| New measurable mechanism | Transforms of exhausted close-only grammar |
| Free reconstructible series | Paid data without EIR |
| Independence / ablation | Multiplicity-unaware grids |
| Understanding without edge | Forced alpha narratives |
