**Result:** descriptive hypothesis **supported** (not a LEDA candidate)

## Hypothesis

Mean `|basis_lag|` is higher under high lagged RV and high `|funding|` than under calm regimes.

## Results

| Regime | n | mean \|basis\| |
|--------|---|----------------|
| high RV (p75) | 423 | **0.000945** |
| low RV (p25) | 932 | 0.000474 |
| high \|funding\| | 687 | 0.000791 |
| unconditional | 2512 | 0.000639 |

High-RV mean ≈ **2.0×** low-RV mean; high-funding also elevated vs unconditional.

## Scientific value

Cross-venue dislocation is **state-dependent**: wider under uncertainty and leverage
stress. Not pure constant USD/USDT rail noise.
